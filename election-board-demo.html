05 · Step 3 · Eligibility and access

Make voting entitlements explicit.

Voter roll

When voting opens, the app creates a list from eligible active primary-owner accounts. The roll provides a basis for tracking participation.

Proxy option

An eligible owner may assign an active member of the same property to submit that owner’s ballot.

Authorization checks

The app checks that the ballot belongs to the election and that the submitting account is the owner or assigned proxy.

Duplicate protection

The submission flow prevents an already-used voter entitlement from being submitted again.

Board discussion: confirm the governing documents, voter eligibility rules, proxy policy, and voter-roll review process before a real election.

09 · Responsible rollout

A pilot should start with the Board’s rules and trust requirements.

Confirm before a real election

  • Governing documents and voter/proxy eligibility
  • Voter-roll verification and correction process
  • Privacy notice, access, and retention policy
  • Security, backup, and recovery review

Prove in a test cycle

  • Sample residents and candidates
  • Admin rehearsal and resident usability test
  • Closeout, tie, and support scenarios
  • Written acceptance criteria and go/no-go
Important: The current data model associates recorded votes with voter and candidate records. Do not describe this implementation as an anonymous/secret ballot or certified election system. Confirm privacy and governance requirements before deployment.
